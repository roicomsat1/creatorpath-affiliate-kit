# Get CreatorPath Africa products found on Google
Page: https://roicomsat1.github.io/creatorpath-affiliate-kit/products/ (links to all 22 Selar products)
1. Open https://search.google.com/search-console and sign in with your Google account.
2. Add property > "URL prefix" > https://roicomsat1.github.io/creatorpath-affiliate-kit/
3. Verify with the "HTML file" method: download the google*.html file and send it to Grok Bot, which uploads it to the repo. Then click Verify.
4. Sitemaps > submit: sitemap.xml
5. URL Inspection > paste the /products/ URL > "Request indexing".
Note: the sitemap lists only our own pages. Google ignores selar.com URLs in a sitemap on another site. Selar product pages get found through the links on our page.
Honest note: Selar and Google decide whether selar.com product pages get indexed, and nothing guarantees it. Our page helps with discovery through backlinks and searches for the brand name. Indexing usually takes days to weeks.
